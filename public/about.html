About Neurolook

Neurolook is powered by NeuroMorpho.Org, the largest library of digitally reconstructed neurons and glia in the world. More than 260,000 three-dimensional tracings have been contributed by over 1,000 laboratories worldwide. When researchers trace the shape of a cell under the microscope, many share that reconstruction through NeuroMorpho.Org, making it freely available for others to study, compare, and reuse.

How Neurolook works

Search & Browse

Query by neuron name or ID, or filter the full library by species, brain region, cell type, and archive.

Compare

Select two or more neurons to view their 3D morphology and metadata side by side.

My Collection

Save neurons to revisit later or export as CSV. Your collection lives only on this local instance.

No sign-in

Neurolook doesn't use accounts. Your collection and recently-viewed history are local to this instance.

Citation Policy

When using any material found on or downloaded from NeuroMorpho.Org in your publication(s), please include each and all three citations listed below:

  • The original paper(s) that describe the reconstructions, which appear(s) as a PubMed link in the detailed metadata page of each reconstruction.
  • The complete name: NeuroMorpho.Org (RRID:SCR_002145).
  • Tecuatl C, Ljungquist B, Ascoli GA (2024) Accelerating the continuous community sharing of digital neuromorphology data. FASEB Bioadv. 6(7):207-221. doi: 10.1096/fba.2024-00048

Every neuron detail page in Neurolook includes a ready-to-copy citation formatted this way.
